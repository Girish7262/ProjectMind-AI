## 1. Purpose

The primary purpose of this platform is to help organizations preserve, understand, and utilize project knowledge efficiently.

The platform enables developers and engineering teams to:
- Reduce project onboarding time.
- Minimize dependency on individual team members.
- Preserve critical business and technical knowledge.
- Improve project understanding through AI-assisted knowledge retrieval.
- Support faster engineering decision-making using contextual project knowledge.
- Reduce the effort required to comprehend large enterprise applications.

The objective is not to replace developers, but to ensure that institutional project knowledge remains accessible regardless of employee turnover or organizational changes.

---

## 2. Why Now?

Modern software projects have become increasingly complex, making it difficult for developers to understand business logic, system architecture, and historical decisions.

Several industry trends make this problem more critical than ever:
- Increasing software system complexity.
- Growth of distributed and remote engineering teams.
- High employee turnover across the technology sector.
- Artificial Intelligence capabilities reaching the maturity required to understand code and documentation.
- Internal documentation being frequently incomplete or outdated.
- Organizations expecting developers to reach productivity rapidly.

These compounding challenges create a strong demand for an AI-powered enterprise knowledge platform.

---

## 3. Product Category

This product belongs to the category of Enterprise AI Knowledge Platforms.

Unlike general-purpose AI assistants, this platform focuses specifically on understanding target enterprise projects and their associated business knowledge.

The platform combines knowledge from multiple enterprise sources and provides project-specific, role-aware responses. Key integration sources include:
- Source Code
- Git History
- Jira
- Confluence
- Business Rules
- Technical Specifications

The platform is designed specifically for enterprise project understanding rather than general AI conversations.

---

## 4. Out of Scope

The platform is not intended to replace existing enterprise engineering tools. 

Specifically, it is not:
- A source code repository.
- A project management tool.
- A documentation platform.
- An Integrated Development Environment (IDE).
- A DevOps platform.
- A general-purpose AI chatbot.
- An AI coding assistant.

The primary focus remains understanding and preserving existing project knowledge.

---

## 5. Product Origin

The product concept originated from real-world experience working on enterprise software projects.

Common challenges observed in the industry include:
- Long developer onboarding periods.
- Incomplete or ineffective Knowledge Transfer (KT) sessions.
- Over-reliance on individual key contributors or senior developers.
- Domain and business logic remaining undocumented and hidden inside source code.
- Outdated or deprecated documentation.
- High cognitive load when understanding large enterprise applications.

These recurring challenges inspired the creation of this platform.

---

## 6. When This Product Becomes Unnecessary

The need for this platform would be significantly reduced if organizations consistently achieved all of the following:
- Continuously and accurately updated documentation.
- Fully standardized and automated Knowledge Transfer.
- Absolute minimal dependency on individual developers.
- Rapid onboarding workflows for new engineering hires.
- Existing developer tooling completely solving project knowledge management.

At present, these conditions rarely exist in large enterprise organizations.

---

## 7. Relationship with Existing Tools

This platform is designed to co-exist with existing enterprise engineering tools rather than replace them.

For example:
- GitHub remains the definitive source of truth for source code.
- Jira continues to manage requirements, tasks, and project tracking.
- Documentation platforms remain the official documentation source.

The platform acts as an intelligent knowledge layer that connects these systems and delivers project-specific answers in a unified, contextual manner.

---

## 8. Product Vision

This product is intended to become a long-term enterprise capability.

The initial implementation focuses on solving knowledge continuity challenges in software engineering organizations.

Once validated, the same underlying methodology can be extended to other knowledge-intensive industries such as:
- Banking and Financial Services
- Healthcare and Life Sciences
- Manufacturing and Logistics
- Insurance
- Government and Public Sector

The long-term vision is to build an Enterprise Knowledge Continuity Platform that enables organizations to preserve, understand, and utilize institutional knowledge across multiple domains.

The platform is envisioned as a foundational enterprise capability for organizational knowledge continuity and AI-assisted decision support.
